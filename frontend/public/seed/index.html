Terms & disclosures

  • The market value of $WOOD is not guaranteed. The $0.0100 figure is a planned listing target, not a promise or prediction.
  • The contracts have been reviewed with automated security analysis. A manual independent third-party audit has not been completed yet. As with any early-stage digital asset, participation carries risk, so contribute only an amount you are comfortable committing.
  • Each tranche unlocks when the team marks its milestone as reached, so timing follows delivery.
  • Contributions are held in the sale contract until the round is closed. Closing the round sends them to the TradeWood treasury, a 2-of-3 multisig Safe, which also sets each milestone unlock. The contract has no fixed end date, no minimum raise and no automatic refund, so you are trusting the team to close the round, deliver milestones and unlock tranches.
