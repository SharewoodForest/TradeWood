The Sherwood Whitepaper Overview

TradeWood.app is the primary DEX and Yield Protocol native to Robinhood Chain (Chain ID: 7777). Built on the principles of zero-friction execution, instant state finality, and yield redistribution to community holders.

1. Zero Gas Routing

By leveraging Robinhood Chain's specialized gas-relayer engine, $WOOD transactions feature 0% protocol gas fees, allowing instant micro-swaps and micro-yield compounding.

2. Deflationary $WOOD Mechanics

A constant 0.1% fee on every exchange swap goes toward buying back $WOOD from open liquidity and immediately incinerating it in the Robinhood burn vault.

3. Merry Men Yield Referral Program

Holders can recruit outlaws to earn a perpetual 5.0% Tier 1 and 2.0% Tier 2 commission on all trading fees generated by their network.
